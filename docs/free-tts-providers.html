Text-to-speech providers with no credit card

Free tiers that could sit behind the Cloudflare Worker as more quota-fallback tiers. "Fit" means how well the provider works with the current setup: a Worker proxy that returns MP3 for chunks of about 400 characters. Researched September 2026. Quality is taken from published comparisons and each provider's reputation. I didn't listen to samples.

ProviderFree allowanceResetsQualityFit with the current Worker
Deepgram directAura-1 / Aura-2 $200 credit on signup, doesn't expire. About 6.7M chars on Aura-2 ($30/1M) or 13M on Aura-1 One-time Good (Aura-2). Clear and steady, a little flat. Built for voice agents, not narration. Aura-1 sounds more synthetic. Best
Same Aura voices you already use through Workers AI, so the voice lists stay as they are. Only the endpoint and key change.
Fish Audios2.1-pro-free No character cap, fair-use throttling only Offer ends Nov 30, 2026 Very high. Close to ElevenLabs on naturalness and expression, and ranks near the top of public TTS leaderboards. Good
Returns MP3. The free period ends in about two months. Requests may be used to train their models.
CartesiaSonic 20K chars/month (1 credit = 1 char) Monthly High. Natural and expressive, with good pacing on long text. Good
Fine for personal use. The free plan has no commercial license. It covers about 4 average articles a month.
Gemini TTSAI Studio key Listed as "Free of charge" through Dec 2026, but rate-limited. The one published figure (2.5 Flash TTS) is 15 requests/day, 3 RPM Daily High. Expressive, and you can steer the style with a prompt ("read calmly"). Pace can drift between requests. Weak
At ~400-char chunks, 15 requests is about 6K chars a day. It returns raw PCM, so the Worker would have to add a WAV header. Latency is several seconds per request.
Speechify APISimba Sources disagree: 50K or 500K chars/month Monthly Good. Natural narration voices, less expressive than Fish or ElevenLabs. Probably fits
I couldn't confirm the number from their pricing page.
Mistral Voxtral TTSExperiment plan Phone verification, no card — High, by Mistral's own comparison against ElevenLabs Flash. I haven't seen independent comparisons yet. Unconfirmed
I couldn't verify that TTS is included in the free plan.
ElevenLabsFree plan 10K chars/month, attribution required Monthly Very high. The reference point the other providers compare themselves to. Poor
The free tier blocks traffic it thinks comes from a proxy or VPN ("Unusual activity detected"). Every request would come from Cloudflare's datacenter IPs, so it would probably get flagged.
Edge TTSunofficial No key, no limit — Good. Microsoft neural voices (Aria, Jenny, Guy). Clean but plainer than the newer models. Poor
Uses the voices from Edge's Read Aloud through a reverse-engineered WebSocket. It could run from the Worker, but Microsoft has broken it more than once, and the terms of use are unclear.

Suggested fallback order

Aura-2 (Workers AI) → Fish Audio (until Nov 30) → Deepgram direct → Cartesia → Aura-1 / MeloTTS (Workers AI) → browser voice

Each provider becomes one more tier in the existing quotaFallback() chain, with one more upstream in the Worker and its key stored as a Worker secret.

Left out

  • Card required: OpenAI, Azure Speech F0, Google Cloud TTS, Amazon Polly, Inworld.
  • No card, but no practical use: Hugging Face ($0.10/month of credit), Groq (no TTS output).
